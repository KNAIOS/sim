\subsection{Protocol and Independent Confirmation}
\rev{The primary comparison uses half-load urban microcell (UMi) NLOS and a common requested-count range of 4--16. This regime was selected in a 20-drop exploration. A subsequent protocol fixed 40 new independent channel-demand drops, the two trained models, ranking/admission parameters, and the primary comparator before generating confirmation data. All prescribed runs are retained; the two samples are analyzed separately without retraining or parameter changes.}

\rev{Sionna 2.1.0 supplies UMi channels using TR 38.901 V16.1 parameters}~\cite{tr38901v16,sionna210}.
\rev{A half-wavelength $8\times16$ single-V omnidirectional panel assigns one eight-element column to each module. BS/UE heights are 10/1.5 m; horizontal UE positions are (25,-12), (35,0), (28,14) m. Unnormalized path loss and shadowing, fixed large-scale geometry, and 2-m/s Doppler define the transfer test. Its .161-m aperture differs from the 12-m geometric training array. Each episode spans 140 slots (7 s). Half load scales nominal demands of 360/460/560 Mbit/s to 180/230/280 Mbit/s, with switching probability .04 per slot.}

\rev{Physical rates average spectral efficiency over 99 frequency bins. Per-bin RZF uses user-total flat-PSD powers. Monotone required-power iteration with 42-step scalar bisections and at most 200 iterations replaces the narrowband solve. Power/service checks retain equal-power fallback; reaching the iteration limit is not a proof of infeasibility. The learned features remain center-frequency-based, whereas the current failure lower bound uses active wideband CSI. All methods share this allocator and (3).}

\rev{The exploration compares BA-RL4, PRS4, budget-aware B-PRS4, current-wideband-informed budget search WB-B4, and full activation. WB-B4 is the primary matched-information reference: its eight-slot assessment uses the current active wideband rate and seven causal center-frequency forecasts, without future or sleeping-module CSI. Confirmation evaluates only BA-RL4 and WB-B4. Each learned seed runs all 40 new drops; the deterministic reference runs each once, giving 120 executions but 40 independent drops.}

\subsection{Discussion and Operating Scope}
\rev{The confirmed gain concerns the full controller in the specified regime. Earlier half-load NLOS exploration gives expanded 1--16 WB-B1 125.99 J at 4.714\% outage; 4--16 is an algorithmic domain, not a hardware minimum. Half-load LOS favors WB-B4 (117.68 versus 119.49 J, zero outage for both). In geometric high-load confirmation, the 2.03\% mean saving has an interval including zero, and Myopic4 uses 1.88 J less than BA-RL4. These contrasts locate the demonstrated benefit rather than support universal search dominance or an essential long-term energy head.}

\rev{The compact-panel test validates transfer to standardized channels, not moving near-field visibility-region dynamics. Fixed large-scale geometry, ideal active CSI, and two training seeds bound generalization. Energy uses analytical hardware settings and excludes controller computation. The confirmed 1.576-J saving per 7 s corresponds to .225 W: an additional controller-power differential exceeding this value would offset the modeled gain. Deployment-level net saving therefore requires measurement.}
